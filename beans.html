    The fine print

    • Beans are added when you confirm that you received a paid order.
    • Your streak counts successful orders in a row. Cancelling an order resets the streak to 0.
    • 5 successful orders in a row unlock the secret menu. Once unlocked, it stays on your account.
    • Secret menu items can only be bought with beans, never with money.
    • Redeemed rewards do not earn beans and do not count toward your streak.
    • If you cancel a reward before you receive it, your beans are refunded.
