Our story

A small cafe with a big heart

Shafra began with one espresso machine, a handful of tables and a simple idea: everyone deserves a warm place to slow down.

We roast and brew in small batches, bake our pastries every morning, and buy our beans from farmers we know by name. Nothing fancy, just honest food and drinks, made with care.

“A good cup of coffee is a small moment of kindness.”

Today, our little cafe is where friends catch up, students study, and neighbors become regulars. Come in, find a seat, and we'll take care of the rest.
